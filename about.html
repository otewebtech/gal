About Us


This is with reference to our introduction of our masterbatch company called M/s Gulf Additives llc, it’s a sister concern of M/s Oman Trading Establishment. We are a 100% Omani Polymer colourant company based out of Rusayl Industrial Estate, Sultanate of Oman.


Gulf Additives manufactures Polyethylene based high quality Whites, Colours, Additives and Combination masterbatches covering nearly all aspects of plastic processing including film, Injection and Blow moulding applications. Gulf Additives masterbatches enhances the aesthetic value of the plastic product and make them look appealing and attractive. They are easy to use, non-dusting and can be incorporated in the end product very easily.


Gulf Additives with its creative approach and innovative ideas in the plastic processing industry, is committed to offer cost effective, knowledge based and customized solutions to various plastic processors in the Middle East Region. Gulf Additives seeks to develop mutually beneficial long-term relationships with its valued customers.
