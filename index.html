Welcome to Gulf Additivies

Gulf Additives LLC is OTE Group’s maiden venture into the manufacturing sector. It started trial production in early 2004. With its state-of-the-art technology Laboratory and production facilities and highly skilled staff it has become one of the leading Masterbatch producers in the region. The manufacturing facilities of Gulf Additives are located in the Rusayl Industrial Estate area of Rusayl in Sultanate of Oman.
